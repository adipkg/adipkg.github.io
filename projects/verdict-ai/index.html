Verdict AI

VerdictAI is an AI tool that uses machine learning to predict the duration and verdict of court cases. This information can be used to prioritize cases more effectively, ensuring that the most important cases are heard first. VerdictAI can also be used to identify cases that are likely to settle, which can further reduce the backlog.
