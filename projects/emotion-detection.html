Emotion Detection Through Audio

Developed a deep learning system that can recognize human emotions from audio data, with applications in human-computer interaction, mental health assessment, and marketing. The system uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify six emotions (sad, surprised, fearful, disgusted, happy, and angry) from audio data by extracting Mel spectrograms from combined and augmented datasets.

Project Objective

To construct and train a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify six emotions from audio data by extracting Mel spectrograms from combined and augmented SAVEE, RAVDESS, and CREMA-D datasets.

Key Achievements
  • 69% training accuracy
  • Advanced audio augmentation techniques
  • Preprocessed audio data with Gaussian noise and standardized waveforms
