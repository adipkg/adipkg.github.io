The Researcher

A brief introduction

I'm a final-year CS student at Woxsen University, specialising in AI and Machine Learning. My work spans trading systems, computer vision, NLP, and transportation safety — I tend to find problems more interesting when the domains are unexpected.

Along the way I've filed a patent, published at a conference, contributed to two book chapters, and received a research fellowship at IIIT Hyderabad. I also founded Udhgam, Woxsen's first national tech festival, and currently lead the Technology Club.

In the summer of 2024 I did a semester at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore. It was good. The problem sets were hard.
