06 · Projects

Things I built to find out.

Deep Learning Research

Rider Intention Prediction

Forecasts two-wheeler manoeuvres (lane changes, turns, stops) from multi-view video and motion patterns. Trained VGG16, ResNet and R(2+1)D on the IIITH ICPR RIP dataset and used confusion matrices to study where each architecture misclassifies.

Natural Language Processing

BERT-Based Verdict Duration Understanding

A BERT model that predicts court case durations to help legal systems allocate resources, with datasets scraped and processed using Beautiful Soup, and a Streamlit interface for visualizations and case confidence reports backed by MongoDB.

Audio Processing

Emotion Detection Through Audio

A convolutional network classifying six emotions from Mel spectrograms (SAVEE, RAVDESS and CREMA-D via Librosa), with Gaussian noise augmentation, 250 epochs of training with early stopping and checkpointing, and 70% training accuracy.

Reinforcement Learning

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Algorithmic Trading

Modular DQN, Double DQN, Dueling DQN and combined D3QN agents in PyTorch for Buy/Sell/Hold policies on interday and intraday Indian NSE stocks, with profit-sensitive rewards, inventory penalties and hold-time costs. The findings grew into the agentic execution system above.
